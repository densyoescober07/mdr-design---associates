METHODOLOGY & EXECUTION

Our 6-Phase Architectural Delivery

A transparent, rigorous, and collaborative roadmap guiding your project from the initial sketch to ribbon-cutting handover.

01
Phase 01

Discovery, Brief & Feasibility

We conduct in-depth site evaluations, topographical surveys, sun-path calculations, and statutory zoning audits while mapping your spatial aspirations.

  • Site analysis & microclimate evaluation
  • Municipal zoning & setback confirmation
  • Programmatic brief development
02
Phase 02

Schematic Design & 3D Massing

Our architects craft conceptual floor plans, 3D spatial volumes, and structural silhouettes to explore natural light, circulation, and form.

  • Concept sketches & volumetric models
  • Spatial layout variations
  • Preliminary material moodboards
03
Phase 03

Design Development & Engineering Synergy

We refine the approved concept into precise architectural plans while coordinating structural, mechanical, electrical, and plumbing (MEP) systems.

  • Dimensional drawings & elevations
  • Structural engineer collaboration
  • Photorealistic 3D visualization
04
Phase 04

Statutory Approvals & Building Permits

We prepare all compliance documentation, environmental reports, and municipal submission sets to obtain all statutory building permits seamlessly.

  • Local authority permit sets
  • Fire safety & accessibility compliance
  • Statutory authority liaison
05
Phase 05

Construction Documentation & Tender

We compile comprehensive construction sets including architectural joinery details, finishes schedules, and bill of quantities for contractor tendering.

  • 1:20 and 1:5 architectural detail sheets
  • Comprehensive material specifications
  • Contractor tender evaluation
06
Phase 06

Site Supervision & Project Handover

Periodic site inspections ensure that construction adheres strictly to architectural drawings, material quality standards, and schedule milestones.

  • Regular on-site architectural audits
  • Quality & tolerance verification
  • As-built documentation & handover
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Client Questions & Advisory

Clear answers on our engagement structure, fees, approvals, and collaboration flow.

What is the typical starting point when working with MDR DESIGNS?

We begin with an Initial Discovery Consultation and Site Feasibility Review. During this session, we review your site parameters, zoning requirements, spatial program, budget envelope, and aesthetic vision to produce a tailored Architectural Proposal.

Do you handle local government building permits and approvals?

Yes, absolutely. Our team prepares all necessary technical drawings, structural engineering coordination, and compliance documentation required by statutory authorities and municipal planning departments to ensure smooth permit approvals.

Can you design sustainable, off-grid or net-zero buildings?

Sustainability is intrinsic to our philosophy. We utilize passive solar modeling, natural thermal chimneys, rainwater retention systems, and low-carbon materials to achieve net-zero energy readiness or certified green building standards.

How are architectural fees structured for custom projects?

Depending on the typology and scale, fees can be structured as a milestone-based fixed lump sum tied to phase deliveries (Schematic, Development, Permitting, Tender), or as a percentage of construction cost. Every proposal provides full transparency before any commitment.
