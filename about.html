Shaping Spaces That Inspire & Endure

We are a progressive multidisciplinary architecture and spatial design practice dedicated to creating thoughtful, sustainable, and timeless built environments.

OUR ORIGIN & EVOLUTION

Rooted in Passion, Defined by Precision

Founded over a decade ago, MDR DESIGNS & ASSOCIATES began with a bold conviction: that architecture should elevate the human spirit while existing in absolute harmony with the natural environment.

From our humble beginnings drafting bespoke residential sanctuaries to managing large-scale civic landmarks and sustainable masterplans, our journey has been fueled by relentless curiosity, rigorous craftsmanship, and collaborative partnership with every client.

Key Milestones Along Our Journey

2011

Studio Founded

Established as an independent architectural studio focused on bespoke sustainable residences.

2016

Civic & Commercial Expansion

Expanded practice into commercial developments and civic urban planning projects.

2021

International Acclaim

Honored with global architecture and sustainability awards for net-zero carbon designs.

Present

Pioneering Future Spaces
